TUNISIAN LABOR CODE (SIMPLIFIED VERSION FOR RAPHAEL AI)

SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT CONTRACTS
- A work contract can be fixed-term or indefinite.
- Indefinite contracts are the default unless legally justified otherwise.
- Fixed-term contracts must have a clear reason.

SECTION 2: TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT
- Dismissal must follow legal procedure.
- Unjustified dismissal may lead to compensation.
- Employee rights must be respected.

SECTION 3: WORKER RIGHTS
- Workers have the right to fair pay.
- Workers have the right to safe working conditions.
- Discrimination in the workplace is prohibited.

SECTION 4: DISPUTES
- Labor disputes can be resolved through labor courts.
- Mediation is encouraged before legal action.
